Kısaca

  • Hesap açmazsın, bize kişisel veri vermezsin. Bizim işlettiğimiz bir sunucu yok.
  • Mikrofon yalnızca sen "Uykuyu başlat"a basınca çalışır. Ses telefonunda analiz edilir.
  • Horlama ve uykuda konuşma anlarının kısa klipleri yalnızca telefonunda saklanır, hiçbir yere yüklenmez, 21 gün sonra silinir.
  • Yaş, boy, kilo ve cinsiyet isteğe bağlıdır ve yalnızca telefonunda durur.
  • Veri paylaştığımız tek üçüncü taraf reklam hizmeti Google AdMob'dur. Reklamsız Premium ile kapatabilirsin.

2. Mikrofon ve ses klipleri

  • Ne zaman çalışır: Mikrofon yalnızca sen takibi başlattığında açılır, sen bitirdiğinde kapanır. Gece boyunca ekran kilitliyken de dinlemeye devam eder; bu sırada iPhone mikrofon göstergesini gösterir.
  • Nasıl işlenir: Ses, telefonun belleğinde anlık olarak ses seviyesine (desibel) çevrilir ve horlama / uykuda konuşma anları bulunur. Sürekli bir ses kaydı tutulmaz.
  • Kliplerin saklanması: Bir olay (horlama veya uykuda konuşma) tespit edildiğinde, o olayın çevresindeki yaklaşık 6–15 saniyelik kısa bir klip uygulamanın özel klasörüne kaydedilir (gece başına en fazla 12). Böylece sabah kendi sesini dinleyebilirsin.
  • Nerede kalır: Klipler yalnızca telefonundadır. Dosyalar uygulamanın korumalı alanında durur, Dosyalar uygulamasında görünmez, bize ya da başka birine gönderilmez.
  • Ne kadar süre: Klipler 21 gün sonra otomatik silinir. Ayarlar > "Tüm verilerimi sil" ile ya da uygulamayı kaldırarak hemen silebilirsin.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · App: Did I Snore? (Turkish: Horladım mı?) · Bundle: com.dereli.horladimmi

In short

  • No account and no personal data handed to us. We run no server for the app.
  • The microphone only runs after you tap "Start sleep". Audio is analysed on your phone.
  • Short clips of snoring and sleep-talk events stay on your phone only, are never uploaded, and are deleted after 21 days.
  • Age, height, weight and gender are optional and stay on your phone.
  • The only third party we share data with is the ad service Google AdMob. Premium turns ads off.
  • You can erase everything at any time with Settings > "Delete all my data".

1. Who we are

The app is developed by drL Yazılım ("we"). Contact: dereli.b061@gmail.com. This text explains what data is handled and how when you use the app.

2. Microphone and sound clips

  • When it runs: The microphone turns on only when you start tracking and turns off when you finish. It keeps listening through the night while the screen is locked; iOS shows its microphone indicator while it does.
  • How it is processed: Sound is turned into a loudness level (decibels) in the phone's memory in real time to find snoring and sleep-talk moments. A continuous recording of the night is not kept.
  • Saved clips: When an event (snoring or sleep talk) is detected, a short clip of about 6–15 seconds around it is saved to the app's private folder (at most 12 per night) so you can play it back in the morning.
  • Where they stay: Clips exist only on your phone, in the app's protected storage. They are not visible in the Files app and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • For how long: Clips are deleted automatically after 21 days. You can remove them immediately with Settings > "Delete all my data" or by uninstalling the app.

Details such as age, height and weight can be considered health-related. They are processed only on your phone; we cannot access them and we do not share them with anyone. You do not have to fill in the profile; the app works without it.

4. Apple Health (optional)

If you switch it on in Settings, each night's sleep duration is written to Apple Health with your permission. The app does not read your Health data. You can turn the permission off at any time in Settings or in the iPhone's Health settings.

5. Notifications and widget

The "Good night" and "Good morning" cards are local notifications created by your phone itself; we use no notification server. The widget reads last night's summary from the app on the same phone to display it; nothing leaves the device.

6. Ads (Google AdMob) and tracking

  • The first nights are ad-free. Afterwards a quiet banner may appear at the bottom of the report, and you may choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock extra content. No ads appear during tracking, while sounds play, or on the welcome screens.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may process the advertising ID (if you allow it), approximate IP-based location, device and app information, ad interactions and crash logs to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. This processing is governed by Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Where required, Google's consent form (UMP) and Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt are shown. You may decline and the app keeps working. Change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy or in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • If you buy Premium, ads are removed completely.
  • Sound clips, your sleep data and your profile are not shared with the ad service.

7. Purchases

Premium purchases are made through Apple (App Store). We never see your payment details; the phone only keeps the "purchased or not" result that Apple reports. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings and use "Restore purchases" to bring back an earlier purchase.

8. Export and sharing

Exporting a report as PDF/CSV or sharing it is entirely your decision; the file goes wherever you send it with the iPhone share sheet. We never receive these files.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We have no analytics or tracking tools of our own and do not profile you.
  • We store nothing about you on a server; there are no accounts.

10. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it (clips for 21 days at most). Settings > "Delete all my data" removes nights, clips, profile and unlocked nights. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its local data. Records written to Apple Health can be deleted separately in the Health app.

11. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Your rights (GDPR and Turkish KVKK)

Because your personal data never reaches our systems, we hold nothing to delete, correct or port; your data is on your device and fully under your control. If you still want to exercise a right (access, erasure, objection) or have a complaint, write to dereli.b061@gmail.com. For data Google processes through ads you can also use your rights with Google. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority, or in Turkey with the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

13. Security

Your data sits inside iOS's app sandbox, protected by your device's encryption. Because it never reaches us, there is no breach risk on our side; we still recommend setting a passcode on your phone.

14. Medical notice

Did I Snore? is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace a doctor's advice. Scores and tips are estimates based on the phone's microphone. If you suspect sleep apnea or any other health problem, please consult a doctor.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. When something important changes, the date on this page changes and, if needed, we will tell you in the app.
